Why Goal Setting?

Build a Plan

Core Skills

Six Skills for Effective Goal Setting

Develop practical skills that help transform goals into consistent action.

Clarity

Understand exactly what you want to accomplish instead of keeping your goal vague.

Measurement

Define a way to determine whether you are making progress toward your goal.

Planning

Create a practical timeline and identify the actions required to move forward.

Action

Turn your plan into consistent actions rather than only thinking about the goal.

Tracking

Regularly check your progress and identify what has been completed.

Adjustment

Modify your plan when circumstances change or your original approach is not working.

Learning Roadmap

Goal Setting Roadmap

Progress from understanding what you want to achieve to building a system that helps you reach it.

01

Discover Your Vision

Think about what you want to achieve in your education, career and personal development.

02

Define the Goal

Turn a general idea into a clear and specific target.

03

Make It Measurable

Decide how you will know that progress is being made and when the goal is complete.

04

Set a Deadline

Choose a realistic time frame that gives the goal structure and direction.

05

Create Action Steps

Break the goal into smaller actions that can be completed one by one.

06

Take Consistent Action

Work regularly on the actions instead of waiting for motivation to appear.

07

Track Progress

Measure what you have completed and identify where additional effort is required.

08

Review & Improve

Review your results and adjust the goal or action plan when necessary.

Goal Setting Tips

Build Better Goal Habits

Good goals become useful when they are supported by consistent action and regular review.

Write Your Goals Down

Writing goals makes them easier to remember, review and convert into action.

Start Small

Break large ambitions into smaller steps that you can realistically begin.

Make Goals Clear

Avoid vague targets. Define exactly what you want to accomplish.

Set Milestones

Use smaller milestones to understand whether you are moving in the right direction.

Track Regularly

Review your progress regularly instead of waiting until the deadline.

Learn From Setbacks

If a plan does not work, review it, make reasonable adjustments and continue.
