Why Financial Literacy?

Understand Your Money

Financial literacy helps you understand income, expenses, saving, borrowing, investing and financial risk so you can make informed decisions.

Manage Money

Understand where your money comes from and where it goes through simple budgeting and tracking.

Build Savings

Learn the importance of saving for planned goals, unexpected expenses and future needs.

Make Informed Decisions

Understand basic financial products, risks and trade-offs before making financial choices.

Core Skills

Six Skills for Financial Literacy

Build practical knowledge for managing personal finances responsibly.

Income

Understand salary, business income, allowances and other legitimate sources of money.

Expenses

Understand essential and discretionary expenses and track where money is being spent.

Budgeting

Create a realistic plan for allocating income toward needs, goals, savings and other expenses.

Saving

Develop the habit of setting money aside for future goals and unexpected needs.

Investing Basics

Learn the basic concepts of risk, return, diversification and long-term investing.

Financial Safety

Recognize scams, protect financial information and think carefully before sharing sensitive data.

Learning Roadmap

Financial Literacy Roadmap

Progress from understanding basic money concepts to making informed financial decisions.

01

Understand Money

Learn basic concepts such as income, expenses, saving and financial goals.

02

Track Income & Expenses

Record money coming in and going out so you can understand your spending patterns.

03

Create a Budget

Plan how available income can be allocated to needs, goals, savings and other expenses.

04

Build Savings

Develop a regular saving habit and identify specific financial goals.

05

Understand Banking

Learn about common banking services, digital payments and account safety.

06

Learn Investing Basics

Understand concepts such as risk, return, diversification and long-term investing.

07

Understand Borrowing

Learn how interest, repayment obligations and borrowing costs can affect financial decisions.

08

Protect Your Money

Learn basic financial safety practices and recognize common fraud and scam risks.

Practice

Financial Literacy Activities

Practice basic financial skills using realistic and educational scenarios.

Monthly Budget

Create a sample monthly budget using a fictional income and list of expenses.

Expense Tracking

Record a week's fictional expenses and group them into different categories.

Savings Goal

Choose a fictional financial goal and calculate how much would need to be saved over time.

Risk & Return

Compare fictional financial choices and identify the relationship between potential return and risk.

Banking Basics

Learn the purpose of common banking services and how to keep account information secure.

Scam Awareness

Study common warning signs of financial scams, fraudulent messages and unsafe requests.

Financial Habits

Build Better Money Habits

Small financial habits can help you develop greater awareness and control over your money.

Track Your Spending

Know where your money goes instead of relying only on memory.

Separate Needs and Wants

Think carefully about whether a purchase is necessary or discretionary.

Set Savings Goals

Give your savings a clear purpose and track progress toward the goal.

Understand Before You Commit

Read and understand the important terms, costs and risks of financial products.

Protect Financial Information

Never casually share passwords, PINs, OTPs or other sensitive account information.

Learn Continuously

Financial products and rules can change, so continue learning from reliable sources.
