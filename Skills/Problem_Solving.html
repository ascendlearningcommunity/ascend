Why Problem-Solving?

Problem-solving helps you understand challenges, find practical solutions and handle difficult situations systematically.

Learning Roadmap

Problem-Solving Roadmap

Progress from understanding a problem to implementing and improving a solution.

01

Understand Problems

Learn how to recognize and clearly define a problem.

02

Collect Information

Gather relevant facts, observations and available information.

03

Find the Cause

Use root-cause techniques to understand why the problem exists.

04

Generate Ideas

Create several possible solutions instead of stopping at one idea.

05

Compare Solutions

Evaluate possible solutions based on practical criteria.

06

Implement

Put the selected solution into action using a clear plan.

07

Measure Results

Check whether the solution actually addresses the problem.

08

Improve

Learn from the result and make the solution better.

Practice

Problem-Solving Activities

Practice solving realistic problems to turn knowledge into a practical skill.

Find the Problem

Take an everyday situation and identify the actual problem instead of focusing only on its symptoms.

5 Whys Challenge

Choose a simple problem and repeatedly ask why to explore its possible underlying cause.

Solution Brainstorm

Generate at least five possible solutions before deciding which approach to explore.

Decision Challenge

Compare multiple solutions using cost, time, resources, benefits and possible risks.

Build a Solution

Choose a small real-world problem and create a simple practical solution or prototype.

Review & Improve

Review your solution, identify weaknesses and improve the result through another iteration.

Study Tips

Become a Better Problem Solver

Problem-solving becomes stronger when you practice with real situations regularly.

Define the Problem Clearly

A clear problem statement makes it easier to understand what needs to be solved.

Do Not Rush to the First Solution

Generate multiple possibilities before selecting one approach.

Look for Root Causes

Try to understand why a problem exists instead of only fixing its visible symptoms.

Break Big Problems Into Parts

Divide a complex challenge into smaller, manageable tasks.

Learn From Mistakes

A solution that does not work can provide useful information for the next attempt.

Practice Every Day

Solve puzzles, coding problems, real-life challenges and decision-making exercises regularly.

Think. Solve. Improve.

Build practical problem-solving skills through structured learning, challenges and real-world practice.
