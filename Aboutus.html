ABOUT ASCEND

More Than Learning.
A Community That Ascends.

ASCEND is a learning and growth community built to help individuals learn, develop skills, discover opportunities and become confident leaders.

Who We Are

From Mission Cadet Upgrade to ASCEND

A Bigger Vision. A Broader Community.

ASCEND evolved from Mission Cadet Upgrade (MCU), a student-driven initiative created by passed out NCC cadets from KASC,Cbe.

What started with a focus on cadet development has grown into a broader platform for learning, careers, skills, technology, business, entrepreneurship and leadership.

Vision & Mission

What drives ASCEND

Our Story

Where the journey began

From Cadets to a Community

Mission Cadet Upgrade began as a student-driven initiative by final-year NCC cadets with the goal of supporting cadets through knowledge, leadership, communication and career awareness.

As the vision expanded, the initiative evolved into ASCEND — a broader community designed for everyone who wants to learn, grow, build skills and lead.
